# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

**Our pledge.** We want participation in Erebus to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or identity.

**Expected behaviour**
- Be respectful and constructive; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith, and help newcomers.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give credit where it is due.

**Unacceptable behaviour**
- Harassment, insults, discrimination, sexualised language or attention, doxxing, or personal attacks.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or publishing others' private information.

**Scope.** This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and to public spaces when representing the project.

**Enforcement.** Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they
judge inappropriate. Report concerns privately through GitHub (use *Report content* on the item, or contact the maintainer
[@blazfxx](https://github.com/blazfxx)). Reports are handled confidentially.
